Wendigo

A smart fan controller built to actually survive a summer under the hood.

In Algonquian folklore, the wendigo is associated with bitter cold and winter. There's a fitting irony in lending that name to a device whose whole job is keeping heat in check.

The aftermarket fan controller market isn't short on options — it's short on ones that hold up. Cheap sensors drift, connectors corrode, and a controller that worked fine on the bench fails quietly two seasons later under a hood full of heat cycles and vibration.

Wendigo is being built around environmental sealing and long-term reliability first, with smart control and app connectivity on top of that foundation rather than instead of it. The goal is a fan controller you install once and stop thinking about.
