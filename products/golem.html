Golem

Prototype Hardware

Power without a leash. A 40A solid-state relay that turns your ECU's slow PWM into the frequency you choose, set from your phone.

In Jewish folklore, a golem is a being formed from raw material and given a purpose — an obedient servant built to carry out exactly what it's directed to do, not the mindless brute later pop culture turned it into. That's the job here: take whatever low-frequency command your ECU is comfortable sending, and carry it out properly — regenerated at a clean, high frequency you choose, up to 40A.

Most aftermarket ECUs cap their PWM output well below what a pump or fan motor actually wants to see, and cheap automotive relays can't switch fast enough to fix that on their own. The result is a low-frequency square wave doing a job it was never suited for — hard on windings, audible, and inefficient.

Golem is a 40A solid-state relay and PWM power module, built for people who run serious loads on aftermarket EFI. It sits between your ECU and the load and works completely on its own, no ecosystem required.

PWM that you control. Golem reads the slow PWM signal from your ECU and regenerates it at the frequency you choose. Fans, pumps and other loads run smoother and quieter, and you set the frequency from your phone.

Two modes in one module. Run it as a straight 1:1 relay, or switch to Enhance PWM. It accepts a 12V trigger or a ground-switched ECU output, so it fits Holley, Haltech and most other setups.

Current sensing built in. Golem measures the current each load draws and reports it live in the Veil app. See what a fan or pump pulls without a clamp meter, and spot trouble before it costs you a part.

Set up from your phone. Configure everything over Bluetooth with the Veil app, no laptop or cables. Settings stay on the module, so it works the same whether or not your phone is nearby.

Safe by design. It powers up with the output off. Reverse polarity protection, transient suppression and an isolated signal input are built in for the realities of a car's electrical system.

Low-loss, high-current switching. A low-resistance power stage handles up to 40A with very little heat, in a compact weatherproof package.

Standalone, but part of a family. Golem works on its own, and it shares the Veil app with other Reaper Controls products, so one app can manage your whole setup.
