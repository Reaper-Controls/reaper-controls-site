About Golem

Most aftermarket ECUs cap their PWM output well below what a pump or fan motor wants to see, and ordinary relays can't switch fast enough to fix it. Golem sits between your ECU and the load and does the job properly: it takes the slow command your ECU is comfortable sending and carries it out at a clean, high frequency, up to 40A.

Set it up from your phone, watch the current your load draws, and leave it. Settings live on the module. First prototype units are built and on the bench, and testing has gone better than we hoped.
