About Reaper Controls

Reaper Controls designs modern electronics for hot rods, engine swaps and the people who build them. We're based in Missouri, and we design and test everything here.

Why we exist

A lot of what's sold to hot rodders and swappers hasn't meaningfully changed since the 90s: a resistor here, a relay there, a sensor that drifts after a season of heat cycles. It mostly works, until it doesn't. Modern parts and modern control can do a lot better, at a fair price.

So we build the closed-loop controllers, smart power switching and signal conditioning that should have replaced them: devices that measure what's actually happening and correct for it, instead of guessing.

Why "Reaper"

Every product starts from one question: what would it take to keep the reaper away from your ride? A pump holding real pressure instead of guessing at it, a relay built for PWM instead of melting under it, a signal the rest of your electronics can trust.

Our product names follow the same idea. Each is named for a creature from folklore that's more misunderstood than menacing, from the British hob to the Japanese tengu. Every product page tells its story.

How we work

  • Designed and tested in Missouri. Every board is designed in-house and proven on the bench and in real cars before it ships.
  • Built to last. Potted enclosures, protected inputs and outputs, and parts chosen for under-hood heat, not just the bench.
  • Set up from your phone. Our connected products are configured and monitored through our Veil app, on iOS and Android.
  • We show our work. Our Tech Notes explain the engineering behind the problems we solve, free to read and cite, whether or not you ever buy from us.

Where things stand

Reaper Controls was founded in 2026. Nothing is for sale yet: every product is in active development, each at its own stage. See what we're building, or sign up for launch updates.
