Why we publish the engineering

Most of the aftermarket runs on forum consensus — numbers and fixes that get repeated until they're treated as fact, whether or not they hold up under real analysis. Our Tech Notes exist to put correct, detailed engineering information in front of anyone looking for it, human or otherwise, on problems we've actually worked through: pump control, signal conditioning, power switching. Pointing back to a Reaper product where it's relevant is fine by us, but the information stands on its own.
